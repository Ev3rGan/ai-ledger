# Preserve published revisions and corrections

Substantive factual, evidentiary, Claim, or summary changes to a published Story or Digest create a new immutable revision and a public Correction Notice instead of silently replacing prior content. Minor non-factual fixes may update the current presentation without a notice, while materially new real-world actions remain related Stories rather than revisions. This adds revision and republication complexity, but preserves public trust, auditability, and reproducible Research Answers.
