# Preserve claim-level provenance

Summaries and research answers will be derived from first-class Claims linked to immutable Evidence Spans from specific Document Versions, rather than storing only generated prose and retrieved chunks. Each Evidence Span records its Claim-relative Evidence Role and is anchored to its immutable Document Version by exact text, offsets, hashes, and available type-specific locators, so that extraction output which rewrites or invents text cannot become evidence. This prevents a first-party announcement, an independent check, a secondary report, and a Community Signal from being counted as interchangeable or independent confirmations. The added modeling and verification work makes citations testable, corrections reproducible, source dependence visible, and conflicts explicit; rebuildable retrieval Chunks are therefore never treated as evidence by themselves.
