# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AI Ledger a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's sensitive information.
- Accept maintainers' scope and moderation decisions in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, or other material that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/Ev3rGan/ai-ledger/security/advisories/new). Include relevant links or screenshots and disclose conflicts of interest. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.
